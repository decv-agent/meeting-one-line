The one sentence that replaces the whole meeting: no agenda, no slides, no "let's circle back" — just the decision, in a single line.

## The line

> This meeting could have been an email, and the email just says: approved.

## Why it works

Every pointless meeting is a decision pretending to need a room. The line skips the room and prints the decision instead — and "approved" is the whole meeting, in one word.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints nothing.
